Daybook – the work diary that writes your report

Daybook is a fast personal work diary and task log. Log daily tasks in seconds, organise them into optional projects, track progress by day, week and month, and export Excel, PDF or CSV reports. Private, offline-ready and free.

Features

  • Quick add: dates, hours, modules, priority, projects and due dates in one line
  • Day, week, month, board and table views
  • Optional projects with keys, boards and work-in-progress limits
  • Analytics dashboard with heatmap, burn-up and insights
  • Excel, PDF, CSV and Markdown reports, plus a standup generator
  • Data stays in your browser; works offline as an installable app
